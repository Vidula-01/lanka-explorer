About Us

We are a student group who built Lanka Explorer as our web development mini project. Our goal is to help travellers discover Sri Lanka and plan a trip with live weather, rupee prices and an interactive map. The site is built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JSON and three free web APIs.
